Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Sarkari Hith, candidate trust is our top priority. We collect minimal personal data strictly necessary to provide customized examination and job alert services:

  • Email Address & Preferences: When you subscribe to our Job Alerts or Track a Job Deadline, we record your email, selected educational qualifications (e.g. 10th, 12th, Graduate, B.Tech), and preferred sector or state.
  • Technical Logs: Standard web server logs including IP addresses, browser user-agents, and timestamp data for anti-abuse and rate-limiting security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for:

  • Delivering verified daily government recruitment notifications and deadline reminders.
  • Enhancing search relevance and user experience across our matrix portal.
  • Protecting our servers from automated scrapers, denial-of-service attacks, and spam.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal email or preference details to any third-party marketers or commercial advertisers.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

Sarkari Hith utilizes browser LocalStorage solely to persist your theme preferences (e.g. Dark Mode) and filter bookmarks locally on your personal device. We do not use intrusive cross-site behavioral tracking cookies.

4. External Links to Official Websites

Our portal links to official government examination portals (.gov.in, .nic.in, ssc.gov.in, upsc.gov.in, etc.). When you navigate via external links, you are subject to the privacy practices and terms of those respective government agencies.

5. Unsubscribe & Data Deletion

You retain full control over your alerts. Every automated alert email contains an instantaneous one-click unsubscribe link. Alternatively, you can email us at sarkariportal7@gmail.com to have your records permanently purged from our database.
